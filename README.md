I build software in Nairobi, mostly for things people here deal with every day: money, learning, farming and getting around.

Right now I run **[Daelus](https://www.daelus.co.ke)**, a small software studio.

### Things I've built

- **[Fungu](https://github.com/ken-muritu/Fungu)**: keep track of money lent between friends, with a due date and an M-Pesa payment request
- **[Pesa Studio](https://github.com/ken-muritu/pesastudio)**: catch, replay and test M-Pesa callbacks while you build
- **[Msingi](https://github.com/ken-muritu/msingi)**: a base for online shops in Kenya, with M-Pesa and WhatsApp
- **[Luminary](https://github.com/ken-muritu/luminary)**: puts Bible verses on the church screen as the preacher reads them
- **[Rewind](https://github.com/ken-muritu/rewind)**: look back on your year, then plan the next one
- **[Primer](https://github.com/ken-muritu/primer)**: learn a tool by asking questions of its own docs
- **[Aurum](https://github.com/ken-muritu/aurum)**: turn videos and articles into a course you can finish
- **[Tafari](https://github.com/ken-muritu/tafari)**: a WhatsApp bot that answers when you tag it
- **[FrameFlow](https://github.com/ken-muritu/frameflow)**: pull the best still frames from a video
- **[Harvestly](https://github.com/ken-muritu/harvestly)**: warns farmers before stored maize goes bad (hackathon team project)
- **[Ma3](https://github.com/ken-muritu/mat3)**: book and track matatus
- **[Foveo](https://github.com/ken-muritu/foveo)**: a reading aid that blurs everything except the line you're on
- **[VELA](https://github.com/ken-muritu/vela)**: share a thought and it goes to the right app (prototype)
- **[SchedulePro](https://github.com/ken-muritu/schedulepro)**: a simple daily and weekly planner

Mostly TypeScript, Python, Flutter and Go.
